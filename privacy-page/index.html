What this installation processes

Benion processes authorized account identifiers, access tokens, publication metadata, comments, conversation messages, Story interactions, assistant preferences and delivery-attempt records. These support account verification, scheduling, conversations, campaign replies and duplicate-send protection. Connecting an account does not automatically activate a campaign.

Storage and service providers

Account data is stored in this operator's private server directory and backups. The dashboard is restricted to the operator. Meta receives the requests needed to provide the selected Instagram features. When the operator enables an AI assistant, relevant conversation context and product information are sent to the configured model provider. Published media and its fetch URLs are accessible as required for publication.

The dashboard uses a session cookie for authentication. Browser-local preferences and legacy simulation drafts may persist on the operator's device. Benion does not include advertising tracking.

Deletion and authorization

Contact the operator above to request access to or deletion of data handled by this installation. Provide enough information to identify the Instagram interaction, without sharing passwords or access tokens. The operator controls retention, backup handling and fulfillment of requests.

Instagram account owners can revoke this app's authorization in Instagram or Meta settings. The operator should stop affected automations before deleting account data. Removing duplicate-send records can change whether an older interaction is considered already handled.

This notice describes the self-hosted application. The operator must keep their contact details and actual service-provider practices current.
